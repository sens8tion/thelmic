# Rap audition: the words

What each clip on BARS-OF-SOAP, SHOUT-OUT and FALA-SERIO says, for picking phrases to chop. The
**beat** is where the line starts in the clip as it plays in the set: the clip's own tempo (170,
or 85 for "gone"), counted from the clip's start. Four beats to a bar, so beat 16 is bar 5.

Heard by a transcriber (faster-whisper `base`, told each track's language), so every line is "heard
as". It hears slang as dictionary words. Where the clip's own name or the Portuguese settles a word,
the likely reading is given and marked *(probably)*. Where it doesn't, the line says **unclear**.
The raw output, with per-word timings and confidences, is in `2026-09-18_rap_audition_words.json`.
